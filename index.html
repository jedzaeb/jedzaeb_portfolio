Based in the Hudson Valley, NY, I'm a versatile performer with extensive credits across film, commercial, theater, and voice work. With training from UCB Improv, Shadowland Theater, and diverse movement and vocal workshops, I bring depth, authenticity, and professional dedication to every project.
